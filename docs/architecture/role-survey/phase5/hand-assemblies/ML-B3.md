# Associate — policy engagement M and E

**Organisation type:** national NGO  


## The role
You support policy engagement in Bihar by turning monitoring and evaluation evidence into briefs, decks and partner conversations.

## What you will do
- You turn Bihar monitoring results into two-page policy briefs for the policy research team each month.
- You co-author evidence briefs with the programme team on named social policy questions before partner meetings.
- You build concept notes and slide decks for proposed Bihar programmes ahead of leadership review.
- You write white papers that state methods, findings and limits for each evaluation question under review.
- You brief state and partner counterparts each quarter on which monitoring indicators shift the live debate.
- You analyse primary and administrative datasets in Bihar and file dated notes with the source and cut-off.
- You design survey modules and feedback tools with field teams so each round tests one named programme claim.
- You keep a tracker of briefs requested, drafts in progress and products cleared for external use in Bihar.
- You refuse any public product that lacks a cited evidence source and a verification date on the cover page.

## Clear outcomes
- Policy briefs and decks reach leadership review with cited, dated evidence and clear recommendations.
- Partners receive a quarterly update that links monitoring findings to the live policy questions.

## Must-haves
- Candidates must show four or more years relevant full-time experience.

## Desirable
- A master's degree with relevant work experience is preferred.

## Selection
Step 1: first round with co-founder; Step 2: assignment; Step 3: second round with other co-founder; applications reviewed on a rolling basis.
