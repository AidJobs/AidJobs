# Communications support officer

**Organisation type:** donor / intergovernmental  
**Reports to:** Director communications  
**Languages:** English  

## The role
From Cambridge you help the Director of communications deliver biodiversity and wildlife-trade communications on a fixed-term grade A3 contract.

## What you will do
- You turn biodiversity and wildlife-trade priorities into dated press releases, web stories and briefs each quarter.
- You update Cambridge website sections and event pages so live campaign calendars match cleared key messages.
- You coordinate public advocacy messages with partner organisations before release and log who signs off each line.
- You draft campaign and advocacy plans that name target audiences, channels and success measures for each theme.
- You edit technical wildlife-trade research into plain English for public, media and partner readers before release.
- You produce simple digital assets and key-message packs for priority themes and file them for country teams.
- You track which communications products shipped each month against the unit workplan and flag slips early.
- You brief the Director of communications every week on cleared advocacy products, media requests and upcoming launches.

## Clear outcomes
- Campaign plans name target audiences, channels and success measures before any public release goes live.
- Website and event pages stay aligned with the live campaign calendar and signed-off key messages.

## Must-haves
- Candidates must show bachelor degree in communications, journalism, marketing or related field.
- Two years experience in communications management, public relations or journalism.
- Candidates must show written and spoken fluency in English.
