# World Heritage monitoring officer

**Organisation type:** donor / intergovernmental  
**Reports to:** World Heritage Conservation Officer  
**Languages:** English, Spanish  
**Team:** World Heritage  

## The role
You monitor natural and mixed World Heritage sites from San José, reporting to the World Heritage Conservation Officer across the annual Committee cycle.

## What you will do
- You produce State of Conservation reports for natural and mixed World Heritage sites on the annual Committee calendar.
- You review environmental and social impact assessments under Operational Guidelines paragraphs 172 and 174.
- You verify site conservation status with regional offices, commissions and technical experts before drafting.
- You organise Reactive Monitoring and Advisory missions, briefing mission experts and reviewing final mission reports.
- You maintain up-to-date geospatial data and monitoring files for World Heritage sites and the World Heritage Outlook.
- You brief the World Heritage Panel on priority site risks so reporting cut-offs are met without late surprises.
- You support technical and financial donor reporting for the World Heritage unit against agreed workplan dates.
- You draft web stories, newsletters and press material on site conservation status with the communications team.

## Clear outcomes
- State of Conservation reports reach the annual World Heritage Committee session on time with cited, dated evidence.
- Each monitored site file holds verified geospatial data, mission findings and named follow-up owners.

## Must-haves
- Master degree in environmental science, natural resource management or related conservation field.
- At least two years relevant experience in nature conservation or protected areas.
- Candidates must show written and spoken fluency in English and Spanish.

## Desirable
- Knowledge of the World Heritage Convention is a strong advantage.
- Proficiency in French as a working language of the World Heritage Convention is desirable.

## Selection
Online profile in the Human Resources Management System; applications after the closing date are not accepted.
