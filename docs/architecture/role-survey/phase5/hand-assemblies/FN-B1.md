# Chief financial officer

**Organisation type:** domestic charity / foundation  


## The role
You lead financial operations, controls and lean systems across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda, keeping multi-country youth programmes audit-ready as the organisation grows.

## What you will do
- You spend twenty percent of effort on financial oversight so multi-country cash positions and donor restrictions stay sound.
- You spend twenty percent of effort building lean country finance systems matched to each programme stage across East Africa.
- You spend twenty percent of effort defining unit-cost and financial metrics with executive and product leaders.
- You spend ten percent of effort on tax, statutory and internal-control compliance across all four country offices.
- You spend fifteen percent of effort leading external funder and board reporting with plain-language variance explanations.
- You spend fifteen percent of effort modernising finance and administration tools so country teams cut cost and manual work.
- You drive swift closure of every external audit recommendation against named owners and dates in the tracker.
- You build and review multi-year proposal and funder budgets with programme and fundraising leads before submission.
- You coach country finance leads on cost discipline so monthly close packs reach leadership accurate and on time.

## Clear outcomes
- Monthly finance packs reach leadership and the board on schedule with clear variances and no unexplained control gaps.
- External audit recommendations close on time against named owners and dates recorded in the compliance tracker.

## Must-haves
- Six to ten years of strategic leadership experience. Specific finance leadership is not required.
- Candidates must show oversight of strategic finance and operational systems.
- Candidates must show financial modelling for lean finance systems.

## Selection
Screening includes a Certificate of Good Conduct check for child and youth safety; applications reviewed against the listed leadership bars.
