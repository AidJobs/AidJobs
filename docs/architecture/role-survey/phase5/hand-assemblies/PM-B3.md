# Program manager — skilling

**Organisation type:** national NGO  


## The role
You lead the one-year residential skilling programme in Gurugram, pairing holistic and artificial-intelligence-assisted learning with mainstream hiring partnerships.

## What you will do
- You run the one-year residential skilling cohort in Gurugram against a weekly delivery calendar with named owners.
- You agree pedagogy, assessment checkpoints and career-readiness targets with the founder before each new intake.
- You convene learners, trainers and executive stakeholders every fortnight and record decisions with owners and dates.
- You reset priorities within five working days when a module slips, and brief the founder on the recovery plan.
- You keep risk logs, attendance and assessment scores current so leadership can see blockers before each review.
- You coach trainers on planning and close-out so every cohort uses the same quality checklist at graduation.
- You connect high-performing learners with mainstream hiring partners and executives for feedback within two weeks.
- You produce a monthly progress pack covering enrolment, completion risk and stakeholder actions for the founder.
- You treat setbacks as recovery plans with owners, so results hold when a module or partner slips mid-cohort.

## Clear outcomes
- Every residential cohort closes against the agreed career-readiness targets and graduation checklist without scope drift.
- The founder receives a dated monthly pack on the first working day covering risks, decisions and learner progress.

## Must-haves
- Candidates must show eight or more years in programme or team leadership.
- Candidates must show track record leading complex multi-stakeholder programmes.

## Selection
Step 1: resume shortlisting; Step 2: screening round; Step 3: final round with Founder; Step 4: office visit for team meet-up; applications reviewed on a rolling basis.
