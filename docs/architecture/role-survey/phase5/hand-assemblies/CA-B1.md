# Senior science communication officer

**Organisation type:** domestic charity / foundation  
**Reports to:** Science communication manager  
**Team:** Corporate and science communications  

## The role
You plan and quality-assure multi-channel science communication, reporting to the science communication manager in the corporate and science communications team.

## What you will do
- You plan multi-channel science communication so each programme names its audience, channel mix and release week.
- You quality-assure science communication outputs before release, checking clarity for non-specialist readers.
- You translate research findings into plain language for public, partner and funder audiences named in the plan.
- You coordinate media and event communications with programme leads at least ten working days before each public moment.
- You mentor junior communication officers each month on drafting, editing and channel scheduling against the checklist.
- You agree visibility roles with partners and programme leads before public release and file the clearance note.
- You prepare communication inputs for funder reports using verified figures from programme teams only.
- You track communication performance after each campaign and share lessons with the science communication manager.

## Clear outcomes
- Public products leave the team in plain language with a named audience, channel plan and release week.
- Funder reports receive communication inputs that match verified programme figures before the stated deadline.

## Must-haves
- Candidates must show advanced communication skills for science audiences.

## Selection
Competency review against the listed criteria; safeguarding background checks form part of screening.

Submit your application through the online application on the vacancy listing.
