# Technical project manager

**Organisation type:** international NGO  
**Reports to:** Vice President, Technology  

## The role
You provide technical project management for the product platform, reporting to the Vice President, Technology, across engineering and data science workstreams.

## What you will do
- You partner with Engineering and Data Science each sprint to plan and execute the product technical roadmap.
- You run planning sessions that balance feature delivery, technical debt and platform stability for the quarter.
- You define milestones, deliverables and success metrics, and track them in the shared project board weekly.
- You manage concurrent development and enhancement workstreams so release dates stay visible to stakeholders.
- You maintain sprint plans and release dates, and flag slipped items to the Vice President the same week.
- You document risks, issues and dependencies, and escalate blockers that need leadership decisions within two days.
- You coordinate testing, security and data-privacy checks with the named teams before each production release.
- You present status, risks and outcomes to technical and business stakeholders in the agreed meeting cycle.
- You keep the software development life cycle (SDLC) checklist current for each release train the platform ships.

## Clear outcomes
- Technical deliverables from partner teams meet the documented requirements before handoff to staff.
- The product roadmap, sprint board and risk log stay current enough for a same-day leadership check.

## Must-haves
- Five or more years managing software or data engineering projects.
- Three or more years with engineering teams using modern software development life cycle (SDLC) practices.
- Two or more years on data engineering, extract-transform-load (ETL) or analytics platform work.
- Proficiency with project tracking tools such as Asana, Jira or Linear.
- Candidates must show uS work authorisation without employer visa sponsorship.

## Selection
Online application; optional self-identification survey; review against the listed experience bars.
