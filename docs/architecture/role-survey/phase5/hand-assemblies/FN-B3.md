# External auditor — project financial audit

**Organisation type:** research organisation  


## The role
You provide an independent external audit opinion on the project financial report for the 2024–2025 project years under a lump-sum United States dollar bid.

## What you will do
- You opine whether the project financial report for 2024–2025 is accurate and based on the project accounting system.
- You test expenditure approval, classification and supporting evidence against the project’s accounting records.
- You test whether procurement followed the organisation’s procurement policy for the period under audit.
- You assess whether the project maintains an effective accounting system for the awards named in the bid.
- You check compliance with the finance and audit clauses in the donor agreement for the same project years.
- You test other donor requirements named in the agreement’s audit attachment and record exceptions with evidence.
- You keep the technical and financial proposal consistent with a single lump-sum United States dollar fee.
- You evidence a valid Yemen audit licence and at least three international-funded project audits in the bid pack.

## Clear outcomes
- The signed audit opinion states whether the financial report is accurate and properly supported.
- Findings on procurement, classification and donor finance clauses are evidenced in the workpapers pack.

## Must-haves
- Candidates must show valid audit licence in Yemen.
- Candidates must show eight years auditing experience.
- Candidates must show external donor project audit qualification.
- Candidates must show at least three international-funded project audits.

## Desirable
- At least two international-funded project audits in the last two years.

## Selection
Technical and financial proposal review for the lump-sum USD bid.
