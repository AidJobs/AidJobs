# Project development manager

**Organisation type:** international NGO  


## The role
You position the Myanmar country programme with donors and lead proposal development under the international staff framework.

## What you will do
- You analyse the Myanmar humanitarian context each quarter and turn findings into fundraising priorities.
- You lead proposal development for the country strategy with programme, finance and logistics colleagues.
- You manage active grants and submit donor narrative and results reports on the dates in each agreement.
- You follow partner grant obligations and flag compliance gaps to the country leadership the same week.
- You coordinate internal departments so each proposal package is complete before the donor deadline.
- You support external communication on grants with accurate figures cleared by monitoring and evaluation.
- You file signed contracts and follow-up actions so the grants desk can retrieve them within one working day.
- You represent the country programme in donor meetings and record agreed next steps for the team.
- You keep a living pipeline of concept notes so the country director can see which donors are in play each month.
- You draft donor budgets with finance so every submission matches the ceiling stated in the call.
- You track proposal win rates each quarter and brief country leadership on which themes to drop or keep.
- You clear partner contribution letters before submission so the grants desk holds a complete package.
- You map donor priorities for Myanmar against the country strategy and update the matrix after each call.
- You coach field colleagues on narrative evidence so reports use verified monitoring figures only.
- You close grant files within five working days of the final report so audits can retrieve the trail.
- You schedule donor visits with logistics and security, and circulate the agenda two weeks beforehand.
- You log every compliance flag with an owner and a recovery date before the next donor reporting cut-off.

## Clear outcomes
- Proposal packages reach donors complete and on time against the country fundraising plan.
- Grant reports use verified figures and meet the reporting calendar in each signed agreement.
- The donor pipeline and compliance log stay current enough for a same-week country-director check.

## Must-haves
- Master level education in international relations development or political science.
- Candidates must show humanitarian proposal development and grants management experience.
- Candidates must show donor systems knowledge and external representation skills.
- Candidates must show ability to coordinate and manage a team.
- Candidates must show previous field experience required.

## Field conditions
- Field travel follows the country security plan; accommodation and food are provided in the organisation guesthouse when on mission.

## Selection
International-framework eligibility check, then review against proposal and field experience criteria.

## Apply
Apply through the organisation careers listing; no fees or banking details are requested.
