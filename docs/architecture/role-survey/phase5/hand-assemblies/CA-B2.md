# Communications and multimedia specialist

**Organisation type:** international NGO  
**Languages:** English, Khmer  

## The role
You produce video, documentary and digital stories for the Phnom Penh programme and publish them on the content management system and social channels.

## What you will do
- You produce video, documentary and digital stories that show conservation work for public audiences.
- You publish finished pieces on the content management system and social channels on the agreed weekly schedule.
- You travel to remote field sites for content when briefed, for periods set with the country team in advance.
- You edit multimedia with Adobe Premiere and related tools to the organisation’s published quality checklist.
- You tell conservation stories that connect communities, authorities, partner organisations and youth groups.
- You work across cultures with authorities and partners after a clear brief on tone, consent and local languages.
- You keep project files for each story: shot lists, consent notes and final exports the team can find the same day.
- You propose three story ideas each month and agree priorities with the communications lead before filming starts.
- You file signed consent notes beside each final export before the piece goes live on any channel.
- You caption finished films in English and Khmer so both audiences can follow the same conservation story.
- You check every public cut for community consent and remove faces that lack a signed release.
- You brief youth groups before shoots so participants know how footage will be used on social channels.
- You archive raw rushes and project files within one working day of return from each remote site.
- You align each story with the country conservation calendar so releases match campaign weeks.
- You test playback on the content management system before the scheduled publish slot each week.
- You debrief the communications lead after every field shoot with a one-page note on contacts and risks.
- You keep a simple tracker of stories in draft, in edit and live so the team can see capacity each month.

## Clear outcomes
- Published stories meet the channel schedule with consent notes filed beside the final export.
- Field shoots return with usable footage and a short note on community and authority contacts.
- English and Khmer captions ship with every public film before the agreed publish slot.

## Must-haves
- Candidates must show bachelor degree in communications journalism or related.
- Candidates must show five years video or digital storytelling experience.
- Candidates must show english and Khmer fluency required.

## Desirable
- Non-governmental organisation or international non-governmental organisation digital communications experience preferred.
- Adobe multimedia tools preferred.

## Field conditions
- Field travel to remote sites follows the country security and consent rules; trip length is agreed before departure.
