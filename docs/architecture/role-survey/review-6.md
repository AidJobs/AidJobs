## New findings

**Must-fix.** A: a live batch's size can be exceeded through redirects (the fetch policy follows up to 20 for a page; `Fetcher` logs one line per call; with a fake redirecting four times the batch counted 20 against 20 while the real total was up to about 163), and a board's redirect can reach any public host. Fix: limit redirects, refuse or do not use a redirect off the starting host, count each hop and each new host's robots.txt, reserve the worst case before each request, test with a fake redirect. B: the form check fails on real ids: 22 contain "/" (IATI), 2,747 of 5,873 look like phone numbers (Australian and EU register numbers), about 0.9% of board-employer hashes are all digits; exempt `org_id`, allow "/", prefix the hash, test these shapes.

**Should-fix.** C: the restriction cases above. D: the archive reader follows redirects (`requests` default); pass `allow_redirects=False` and count a 3xx as a failure. E: remove "over" from the skip list and apply the skip inside the experience-first branch. F: held-out adverts can leak through near-duplicates (a later copy with a better rank keeps the copy and drops the held one); give held ids the top rank. G: chrome removal should keep any line matching a known standard text or a fact, inclusion or restriction pattern. H: names beginning "UN " are UN; the UN foundations are not; `row_still_allowed` should check the row's organisation and refuse to run without the held list. I: "maîtrise" only as a degree in context; "licence de conduire" is not a degree. J: tests for the 429 and failure streaks, the per-row re-check, state reloading, cached robots refusals, the "needed" count, redirect counting, the next-page-number check, the R5 gate, the UN drop in `measure`, held-out adverts outside the cap, two "-at-" in a slug, an " at Employer" copy with slightly different text.

**Minor.** The archive reader's 429 and failure streaks are in memory only; `confirm.Fetcher` logs after the request, so an interrupted request is not counted; `comparable_title` cuts every source's title at " at "; "(M/F/D)" not a both-sexes mark; "About the Candidate" unclassified; `links_in` accepts `http://` next pages on the board's host.

## Fixes (11 November 2026, before the re-review's own fixes were reviewed)

`fd3b0ed`, made before this re-review reported, already exempted `org_id`, counts and snapshot labels from the phone check. Then all findings taken; 75 tests pass (58 before), ruff clean, the form check passes on the real output; a focused re-review of these fixes follows.

- **A:** a live request lets the fetch policy follow at most 2 redirects (`LIVE_REDIRECTS`, through the policy's own `max_redirects`); before each request the worst case is reserved (the page, a new host's robots.txt, and per redirect the hop and a robots.txt read); after it, each hop and a robots.txt read per hop are logged and counted, and a redirect limit counts every hop; a page that ends on another site is not kept, its host is blocked, and it is neither a refusal nor a failure. Tests with fake redirects.
- **B:** the `org_id` form allows "/"; board-employer ids carry a letter before the hash; tests with real id shapes (IATI with "/", Australian and EU register numbers, the hash).
- **C:** restrictions are read by `restrictions_of`, which drops an age match about the people served (children, youth, women and others), "open to all nationals" and "nationals and non-nationals", and a service "for women only"; "between N and M years old", "N years or younger", "Age: N–M", "for women only" and "must be an EU citizen" are found; `national_post` is case-insensitive; height needs a requirement on the applicant; bare "presentable" dropped.
- **D:** the archive session does not follow redirects; a 3xx counts as a failure; the 429 and failure streaks are kept on disk across runs.
- **E:** "over" no longer skipped; the skip and "years ago" checks look at the number itself in both branches; the search restarts after each number, so a skipped one cannot hide the next.
- **F:** held-out adverts are always the copy kept by the near-duplicate step, using the saved draw.
- **G:** a short line that carries a known standard text, a fact, an inclusion statement or a restriction is never site furniture.
- **H:** names beginning "UN " are UN (not UN Global Compact networks or "Un Ponte Per"); the UN foundations are not; `fetch-archive` refuses to run without the plan's held hosts and organisations and re-checks each row's organisation.
- **I:** "maîtrise" counts as a degree only as "maîtrise en" or "diplôme de maîtrise"; "licence de conduire" is not a degree.
- **J:** tests added for the 429 and failure streaks, redirects not followed, the per-row re-check in `fetch-archive`, state reloading, a cached robots.txt refusal, the reserved worst case, redirect counting, the next-page number, the R5 gate, the UN drop and held-out adverts beside the cap in `measure`, two "-at-" in a slug, and an " at Employer" copy with slightly different text.
