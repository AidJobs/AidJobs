# Communications Officer

**Location:** [duty station]  
**Reports to:** [communications / country manager]  
**Contract:** [type and length]

## Role purpose
Plan and deliver clear communications for [audience] so programme results, risks and calls to action are understood and usable.

## Key responsibilities
1. Set the [period] communications priorities for [audience], linking messages to programme results.
2. Brief journalists on [story] with approved talking points and track coverage within [days] days.
3. Run the [campaign] social plan for [channel], with content calendar, moderation rules and weekly metrics.
4. Prepare partner updates for [donor] each [frequency] on results, risks and upcoming milestones.
5. Draft and clear press materials on [story] for [outlet], with quotes, facts and embargo timing.
6. Keep brand voice consistent across [channel] for [campaign], using the approved messaging sheet.
7. Report [metric] for [channel] each [frequency] and recommend one change when targets are missed.
8. Advise [executive] on communications risks for [issue] before public statements are cleared.

## Essential requirements
- Professional communications experience in a mission-driven organisation.
- Experience clearing media or public materials with technical owners.
- Experience planning content for at least one digital channel with metrics.
- Strong writing and editing in [Language].
- Judgement on when to escalate reputational risk.

## Desirable
- Advocacy or campaign experience on [issue].
- Photography or short-form video production.
