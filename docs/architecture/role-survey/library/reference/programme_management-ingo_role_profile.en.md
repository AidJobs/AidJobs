# Programme Manager

**Location:** [duty station]  
**Reports to:** [line manager]  
**Contract:** [type and length]  
**Team:** [number] direct reports  

## Role purpose
Lead delivery of [project / portfolio] so agreed results reach [people / communities] on time, within budget and to the organisation's quality and safeguarding standards.

## Key responsibilities
1. Deliver [project] activities across [sites] to the agreed workplan, quality checks and timeline.
2. Coordinate with [counterpart] on [topic] at least [frequency], recording decisions and follow-up owners.
3. Lead a team of [number] staff on programme delivery, with clear priorities, coaching and performance feedback.
4. Track [indicator] for [project] each [frequency] and flag variances to the manager within [days] days.
5. Prepare the [project] budget and revisions with finance, matching activities to budget lines.
6. Submit [donor] narrative and results reports by [deadline], using verified figures from MEAL.
7. Maintain the risk register for [risks], updating likelihood and mitigations each [frequency].
8. Meet community representatives in [location] on [topic] at least [frequency], documenting feedback for the team.

## Essential requirements
- Demonstrated experience managing multi-site programme delivery at coordinator or manager level.
- Experience supervising staff and working with government or partner counterparts.
- Comfortable owning donor narrative reporting with verified results data.
- [Language] at [level] for coordination with [audience].
- Willingness to travel to [sites] as required.

## Desirable
- Experience with [sector] programming in similar contexts.
- Familiarity with the organisation's project management standards.
