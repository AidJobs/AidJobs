# MEAL Officer

**Location:** [duty station]  
**Reports to:** [MEAL / programme manager]  
**Contract:** [type and length]

## Role purpose
Make monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning usable for [project]: clean data, clear indicators and findings that managers can act on.

## Key responsibilities
1. Check [dataset] quality for [indicator] each [frequency], correcting gaps before reports leave the team.
2. Monitor [project] against the indicator plan each [frequency] and brief the manager on off-track results.
3. Configure and maintain [tool] for [project] data entry, with user guidance and access control.
4. Define and track [indicator] for [outcome], with baseline, target and data source named.
5. Prepare the [audience] MEAL brief each [frequency] with verified figures and plain-language findings.
6. Apply agreed confidentiality rules to [dataset], including access lists and secure storage checks.
7. Facilitate a learning session on [topic] after [milestone], capturing lessons and owners for follow-up.
8. Engage [stakeholder] on findings from [study] at least [frequency], adapting messages to their decisions.

## Essential requirements
- Hands-on MEAL or M&E experience on multi-site programmes.
- Experience designing or applying indicator plans with baselines and targets.
- Experience with [tool] or a comparable data system.
- Clear writing for non-specialist managers.
- [Language] at [level].

## Desirable
- Experience facilitating after-action learning with programme teams.
- Familiarity with accountability to affected people feedback loops.
