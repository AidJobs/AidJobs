# Finance Officer

**Location:** [duty station]  
**Reports to:** [finance manager / country director]  
**Contract:** [type and length]

## Role purpose
Keep [entity] finances accurate, timely and usable for managers and donors: clean records, controlled payments and reports that reconcile to the ledger.

## Key responsibilities
1. Keep accounting records for [entity] complete and coded to the chart, ready for the [frequency] close.
2. Complete bank and cash reconciliations for [account] each [frequency], investigating differences the same day.
3. Close the books for [entity] each month by [day], including reconciliations and the management pack.
4. Review payment requests for [entity] against budget and supporting documents before approval routing.
5. Prepare [donor] financial reports for [grant] by [deadline], reconciling to the ledger before submission.
6. Maintain the [horizon] cash-flow forecast for [entity] and flag shortfalls [days] days ahead.
7. Prepare the internal audit pack for [scope] by [deadline], with samples and management responses.
8. File [filing] for [entity] by the statutory deadline and keep working papers for audit.

## Essential requirements
- Accounting or finance operations experience in a comparable organisation.
- Experience with month-end close and bank reconciliations.
- Experience preparing donor or grant financial reports that reconcile to the ledger.
- Careful document control for payments and audit packs.
- [Language] at [level] for working with [audience].

## Desirable
- Experience with [system] or a comparable finance system.
- Experience supporting external audit fieldwork.
